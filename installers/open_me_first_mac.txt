Opening Fisheye StitchR on Mac

Fisheye StitchR is free and not yet notarized by Apple, so macOS shows a
security warning the first time. This is normal and safe. To open it:

1. Drag Fisheye StitchR into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click it. macOS says it cannot be opened. Click Done.
3. Open System Settings (Apple menu, top-left).
4. Go to Privacy & Security and scroll down.
5. Find the line about Fisheye StitchR being blocked and click Open Anyway.
6. Enter your password or use Touch ID, then click Open Anyway again.

You only do this once. After that it opens normally.

If macOS asks whether Fisheye StitchR may access your Desktop, Documents,
Downloads or an external drive, click Allow. It needs that to read your photos.
